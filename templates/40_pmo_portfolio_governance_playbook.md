# PMO Portfolio Governance - 14-Phase Operating Model
> Reusable guide inspired by the phases and workshops in an uploaded PMOForge package. No original project names, personnel, budgets or approval records reproduced.

## Objective
Align demand, portfolio selection, delivery standards, resources, risk, dependencies, KPI, benefits, evidence and decision rights. A dashboard alone is not a governance model.

## Portfolio governance
Define investment board, sponsor, PMO leader, project sponsor, delivery owner, resource owner, finance partner and compliance reviewers. Document who reprioritizes and approves exceptions.

## Demand intake
Every proposal identifies objective, business case, owner, dependence, risk, resource assumption, expected benefit and evidence. Priority scores depend on approved weighting and are not automatically authoritative.

## Stage gates
SG-0 Idea/Demand, SG-1 Initiation, SG-2 Planning, SG-3 Execution Checkpoint, SG-4 Pre-Delivery, SG-5 Closure. Each gate has inputs, pass/conditional/hold decision, evidence and approver.

## Portfolio risk and resource controls
Distinguish risk, issue, assumption and decision. Document dependencies directionally, with owner and needed-by date. Track capacity separately from allocation and confirmed availability.

## Financials, KPI and benefits
Define numerator, denominator, formula, units, frequency, owner, baseline, target and audit trail. Separate expected benefits from proven results.

## Facilitation and evidence
For every workshop: pre-read, objectives, role-based attendees, discussion, decisions, outstanding issues and signed outputs. Regulatory crosswalks are candidate mappings until qualified experts validate applicability.

## Handover and value
Publish charter, registers, scorecards, decision logs, evidence catalog and regular benefits review. Never assert maturity improvement without comparable baseline and observation.
