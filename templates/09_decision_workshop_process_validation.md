# Decision Workshop — Process Validation

Reusable anonymized slide-by-slide template for a 60-minute decision-maker workshop.

## Slide 1 — Process Validation Workshop
Validate or correct proposed principal process mapping and interfaces for an inventoried scope.

## Slide 2 — Why validate now?
Before / New reference / To decide. A validated taxonomy does not automatically determine ownership.

## Slide 3 — Our playing field
Use placeholders for document count, folders, branches, total volume and HOLD items. Separate security, HOLD and technical decisions.

## Slide 4 — Official process reference
Six fictional sub-processes PROC-01 through PROC-06. Classification canvas, not lifecycle.

## Slide 5 — Proposed principal mapping
Regulatory watch, objectives, governance, operations, performance, communication. RECOMMENDED / TO VALIDATE.

## Slide 6 — Interfaces to validate
Principal process in center; People, Risk, Legal and Facilities as contributory interfaces.

## Slide 7 — Five key decisions
Governance; crisis; equipment; sensitive cases; out-of-core/ambiguous content.

## Slide 8 — Decisions not frozen here
HOLD, security, technical M365 architecture, archive/ambiguous.

## Slide 9 — Agenda (60 min)
0–5 frame; 5–15 reference; 15–35 mapping; 35–50 arbitrations; 50–55 interfaces; 55–60 closure.

## Slide 10 — Expected outputs
Confirm/modify/analyze; process and sub-process; owner and comment; update Word/Excel after validation.

**Boundary:** Validate the mapping model, not the SharePoint architecture.
